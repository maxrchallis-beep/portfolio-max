About Me

Hello! I'm Max Challis, a passionate and creative designer with a deep love for bringing ideas to life through visual storytelling and just simply making things look cool. Currently studying a communication graphic design degree at Otago Polytechnic in Dunedin, New Zealand. I come from a Computer Science degree in University which I quickly figured out this was not for me, which in turn lead me to take the leap of giving design a go, doing the Level 4 Creative Digital Design Course, completing it and thoroughly enjoying it.


I have always been very much into design especially on computers, from a young age I was designing posters on my family’s home computer of various sportspeople. I have always been interested in making graphics of famous celebrities and editing the images and adding certain features to the image to make it look cooler to myself.


My design journey began as a kid making fun little graphics at home and has definitely been an interesting adventure ever since. My design philosophy revolves around the belief that great design isn't just about aesthetics; it's about solving problems and enhancing user experiences. I'm committed to delivering not only visually stunning work but also functional and effective solutions, though it does help when it looks good doesn’t it? I've honed my skills in on 6 different design types on this portfolio:
Graphic Design
Animation
Web Design
Laser Cutting
Typography
3D Modelling


Recently, I've had the privilege of studying design, partaking in small class design projects as well as big proper assessments each of which has added a unique layer to my creative palette. Familiarising and becoming a pro on Adobe design applications like Photoshop and Illustrator to name a few, I've gained valuable insights and skills that continue to shape my design approach. My Goal for my current course is to gain the design skills to find a job in the workforce in design at the end of my study.


I'm excited to share my portfolio with you, showcasing all of my projects whether it be a fun at home project or a well thought out and planned polytechnic project. Feel free to explore my work and get in touch if you have any questions or if you'd like to collaborate on your next design endeavour. Let's turn your ideas into exceptional visuals together!


Thank you for visiting my portfolio, and I hope you enjoyed it. :)
